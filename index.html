    Model Notes / Limitations

    Baseline: Dijkstra with the same A/B and selected objective, no disruptions, Basic model (zero delays). The percentage compares the selected algorithm’s time or distance with that baseline. “Optimal route changed” compares Dijkstra edge sequences; equal-cost alternatives may exist.

    Disruptions affect both available directions of a physical road section. Ordinary sections stop at junctions and are capped at 200 m. Connected, identifiable bridges, tunnels, causeways, ramps and roundabouts use whole tagged sections. Missing tags and separately mapped carriageways may require separate markers.

    This simplified car-road model is an investigation tool, not driving directions. Missing speed limits use road-type assumptions; speeds do not represent live traffic. Conditional access and complex turn restrictions are not fully modelled.

    Intersection delays are user-set assumptions, not empirically established values. No delay is added at the destination. Car animation is visual only and compresses the modelled trip into approximately 10–20 seconds.
